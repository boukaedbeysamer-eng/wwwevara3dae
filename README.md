# Route Keepsake Co.

Build an e-commerce website for Evara3D FZC, a startup that turns athletes' Strava running routes into custom 3D-printed topography map frames.

What we sell:
Our core product is a personalized 3D topographic relief map of a customer's running route (uploaded from Strava or any others applications that supports gpx files), 3D-printed and mounted in a frame, alongside the athlete's run stats (distance, elevation gain, date, run time, location name). Think of it as a piece of wall art that turns a meaningful run — a marathon, a trail race, a personal milestone — into a physical keepsake.

Pages needed:

Home — Hero section showing a framed 3D topo map product shot, tagline around "Turn your experience into memory," brief explanation of how it works (Upload your Strava run → We generate your 3D terrain → Custom-framed and shipped to your door), featured products, and social proof/testimonials section.

Shop/Products — Grid of available frame sizes/styles and pricing tiers (e.g., Small, Medium, Large frame; Wood vs. Black vs. white). and choose color of the 3d printed map.

How It Works — Step-by-step explainer with visuals: connect Strava, select your run, preview your 3D map, customize frame/text, checkout.

Product Detail Page — Shows a sample map, frame options, size selector, price, "Connect Strava" or "Upload GPX" call-to-action, and an order form capturing run details to be engraved/printed (athlete name, run title, distance, elevation, date).

About — Story of Evara3D FZC, inspired by the UAE trail running community i the UAE (Trail Runners DXB,, and the wider UAE trail scene), founded by Samer Bou Kaedbey (CEO) and Rona Salman Evara talented designer, established June 2026, Dubai/UAE based.

Contact — Simple contact form + email/social links.

Design direction:

Brand colors: Navy blue (deep, premium) as the primary color, with vibrant orange as the accent/CTA color.

Visual motif: Topographic contour lines (the thin elevation lines you see on terrain maps) woven subtly into the background, section dividers, and hero graphics — almost like a watermark/pattern that ties back to our actual product.

Typography: Clean, modern, slightly technical/outdoorsy feel — like a premium outdoor gear or cartography brand, not overly playful.

Overall feel: Premium, minimal, adventure-meets-craftsmanship — similar to brands like Patagonia or a high-end map/print studio, not a generic e-commerce template.

Functionality:

Product catalog with variants (size, frame color/material)

Shopping cart and checkout flow

Order form to collect Strava run data / GPX upload per order

Responsive, mobile-friendly design

Newsletter signup in the footer
